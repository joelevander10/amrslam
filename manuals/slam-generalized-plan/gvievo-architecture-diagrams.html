Base & Motion Control

ROS 2 processes on the same industrial PC
Authority + command muxDiff-drive kinematics Wheel odometryIMU bias + EKF

Produces 50 Hz wheel commands under supervisor leases, physical-panel authority and freshness watchdogs. There is no separate motion MCU; each drive closes its own fast current and velocity loops.

Actuation · BLV-R Drivers ×2

CANopen · CiA 402 profile velocity
Node 1 · LeftNode 2 · Right

Each drive closes its internal current and velocity loops, then returns statusword, velocity and encoder position. Loss of the PC heartbeat forces a drive fault.

Geared Wheel Motors ×2

30:1 gearbox · 4,000 r/min motor ceiling

Left and right geared motors create differential motion. Their encoders are motor-side, so ground slip and gearbox backlash are not measured directly.

Power & I/O Distribution

Distributes traction and auxiliary power and connects the CANable 2.0 / SLCAN adapter, Ethernet links and Modbus I/O island.
