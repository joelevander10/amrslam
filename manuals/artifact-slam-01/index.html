Inside slam_toolbox

Graph SLAM loop · one pass per keyframe scan · slam_mapping.yaml

Front end · where am I relative to the last scans?
Back end · keep the whole graph consistent
Step 1

Keyframe gate

A gated scan becomes a keyframe only after 0.2 m or 0.2 rad of travel, and at most every 0.2 s.

Step 2

Odometry prior

Reads odom → base_footprint at the scan's timestamp: the EKF's guess of how far the robot moved.

Step 3

Scan matching

Slides the scan over a map of the last 10 scans: ±0.25 m at 1 cm, ±20° at 2°, then 0.2° fine.

Step 4

Grow the graph

Adds the matched pose as a node, plus an edge to the previous node carrying relative motion and its covariance.

Step 5

Loop closure

Searches old nodes within 3 m. A chain of ≥ 10 scans must match: coarse response ≥ 0.35, then fine ≥ 0.45.

Step 6

Optimise

Ceres moves every node at once to minimise total edge error. Levenberg–Marquardt with a sparse Cholesky solve.

Step 7

Publish

Re-renders the 5 cm occupancy grid at 1 Hz and publishes map → odom at 20 Hz to cancel odometry drift.

Survey lifecycle mapping_session_node wraps the loop above

The math

Pose-graph optimisation

x* = argmin Σ eᵢⱼᵀ Ωᵢⱼ eᵢⱼ x (i,j) eᵢⱼ = zᵢⱼ ⊖ (xᵢ⁻¹ ⊕ xⱼ)

x are the poses (x, y, θ), z is the relative pose measured by scan matching, and Ω is its inverse covariance. A confident edge pulls harder.

Each Levenberg–Marquardt step

(JᵀΩJ + λI) Δx = −JᵀΩe

A large λ takes small, cautious steps and a small λ approaches Gauss–Newton. JᵀΩJ is sparse, so sparse Cholesky solves it. There is no robust loss, so one false loop edge counts in full.

Scan-match response

score(p) = penalty(p) · 1/N Σₖ M̃(T_p · bₖ)

bₖ are the beam endpoints and M̃ is the occupancy of recent scans smeared by 0.1 m. The penalty shrinks the score the further p strays from the odometry prior (distance 0.5, angle 1.0).

search windows: correlation 0.5 m @ 0.01 m · loop 8 m @ 0.05 m · angles 0.349 / 0.0349 / 0.00349 rad

Localizing on a saved map

AMCL particle filter · readiness gate · production runs

nav2_amcl · one cycle per 5 cm or 0.03 rad of travel
Predict

Move the particles

differential motion model

Shifts every particle by the EKF odometry increment, plus noise scaled by α₁–α₄ = 0.1.

Particle cloud

500 – 2 000 pose hypotheses
Weight · likelihood_field_prob

Score against the map

Scores 180 beams by distance to the nearest mapped wall; beam skipping ignores unmapped clutter.

p(zₖ) = z_hit · N(dₖ; 0, 0.1²) + z_rand / z_max
Resample

Keep the likely ones

KLD-adaptive · every 2 updates

Draws a new set in proportion to weight. The count adapts between 500 and 2 000 (error 0.05, 99 % confidence).

The best cluster gives the pose and its covariance.
